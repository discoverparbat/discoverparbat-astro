Who We Are

A Local Trekking Company from Parbat, Nepal

Discover Parbat is a small, locally run trekking company from Parbat district, just west of Pokhara in Nepal. We started in 2017 as a page sharing the hidden places of our home district, spent 2020 walking and mapping new trails with local guides, and became a full trekking company in 2022. Every trek we run is led by guides who grew up on these ridges and in these villages.

We are the team behind the Kokhe Danda Trek, a quiet homestay route to the highest point of Parbat, through the Magar villages of Lespar and Haljure. Beyond our home trail, we guide offbeat routes like the Khopra Ridge Trek facing Dhaulagiri and the remote Api Himal Base Camp Trek in Far-West Nepal, along with classic Annapurna treks such as Poon Hill, Mardi Himal and Annapurna Base Camp.

Our treks put village life and homestays first, with the mountain views as the reward. Groups stay small, meals are locally sourced, and the money you spend stays in the communities you walk through. If none of our routes fit your dates, we can plan a custom trek around you.

March – May

Spring — Prime Season

Rhododendrons blanketing the ridges in crimson and pink, crisp skies, and the clearest mountain views of the year. April is our most beloved month on the trail.

🍂
October – November

Autumn — Peak Season

Post-monsoon air brings razor-sharp visibility and golden light on the peaks. Dry trails, warm days, and cool starlit nights — the classic Nepal trekking window.

🌿
June – September

Monsoon — Raw & Lush

The trails come alive with roaring waterfalls, thick jungle greenery, and wildflowers you won't see any other time of year. Fewer trekkers, dramatic skies, and an intimacy with the forest that peak season simply can't offer. Best suited for experienced trekkers who don't mind wet boots.

❄️
December – February

Winter — Hidden Gem

December is one of our best-kept secrets. The crowds are gone, the air is crystal clear, and snow-dusted ridges make for extraordinary views. A quieter, more personal kind of trekking experience — and often our guides' personal favourite.
